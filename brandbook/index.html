Brand Identity

Who We Are

RapLegends is a competitive 1v1 brawler where every hit feels legendary. The visual language pulls from the dark-premium mobile aesthetic of card-battlers and the visceral impact design of classic arcade fighters.

⚡
Electric
Every UI element crackles with energy. Glow effects, neon accents, and high-contrast builds create a sense of live power.
🌑
Dark Premium
Deep navy-purples as the canvas. Darkness makes neons pop. Never pitch black — always a purple undertone.
💥
Impact
In-fight UI is bold, oversized, and readable at a glance. Hit counters, round announcements, and KO text are cinematic events.
🃏
Collectible
Characters and cosmetics are presented as rarified cards. Rarity tiers create a progression aspiration language.

VFX Language

Hit Effects & VFX

Borrowed from the Street Fighter / Tekken visual grammar. Every hit type has a distinct effect language that communicates damage weight instantly.

💥
Normal Hit
Yellow-white spark burst. Small radius, sharp edges. 8–12 frame duration. Accompanied by 4–6 debris particles.
🔥
Heavy Hit
Orange-red impact ring with radial shockwave. Larger radius, screen hitstop 2–4 frames. Screen edge flash white.
⚡
Special Move
Cyan-white energy arc. Trails behind limb movement. Lingers 20 frames with fade. Character briefly silhouettes white on contact.
🛡️
Block / Guard
Blue-white hexagonal barrier flash. No knockback particles. Subtle ring pulse expands from player center. Short 6-frame anim.
✨
Ultimate Hit
Purple-magenta explosion. Full screen desaturate → flash purple. Slow motion 0.3× for 12 frames. Debris hangs in air.
💀
KO Finish
Full-screen vignette red flash. Camera shake 3 frames. Loser ragdoll + dust cloud. Time slows to 0.1× for K.O. text entrance.

Motion Principles

Animation & Motion

Motion must be purposeful and fast. Mobile users expect instant feedback. UI transitions are snappy — content reveals are smooth.

Micro-interactions

Button press: scale 0.95 on tap, spring back. Haptic feedback on primary actions. Duration: 80–120ms.

cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)

Screen Transitions

Slide in from right for forward nav. Fade + scale 0.96→1.0 for modal open. Reverse for back. Duration: 220–280ms.

ease-out · 260ms

Card Reveals

Staggered entrance: each card delays 40ms from previous. Scale 0.8→1 + fade in. Rarity glow pulses on arrival.

stagger: 40ms · duration: 300ms

Rarity Unlock

Card flips face-down → face-up with glow burst. Duration scales with rarity: Common 400ms → Mythic 1200ms full ceremony.

flip + particle burst · 400–1200ms

Fight Announcements

ROUND / FIGHT text: scale 2.0→1.0 with bounce, linger 1.2s, then scale 1.0→1.4 + fade out. Camera zoom subtle pulse on FIGHT.

spring · scale 2→1 → fade

Combo Counter

Number pops to 1.3× scale on increment, springs back. Color shifts smoothly as tier escalates. Max combo shakes at ±3px.

pop · 80ms · color lerp

Hitstop (Engine)

Normal hit: 2-frame freeze. Heavy hit: 4-frame freeze. Ultimate: 8-frame freeze + 0.3× slowmo for 12 frames after.

frame-locked · 60fps base

Screen Flash

White flash on KO: 1 frame full-white → 4 frame fade. Red vignette eases in over 200ms. Never instant-off — always eased.

1 frame flash · 200ms fade

Loading States

Pulsing purple ring animation (the RapLegends logo mark). Skeleton cards in neutral dark color shimmer left-to-right.

shimmer · 1.2s loop · ease-in-out

Icon System

Icon Language

Solid-fill icons at 24×24dp. Stroke-weight icons are NOT used — too thin for dark backgrounds at small sizes. All icons should have a subtle glow treatment on active state.
